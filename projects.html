Catalina 30 Sailboat Electric Conversion

Electric motor in the engine bay

Me painting the boat in 2020

Captain Jack at the helm

Converted Falmari, a Catalina 30 tall rig sailboat from a 25hp Universal diesel engine to electric using the ThunderStruckEV sailboat kit. This kit came with a 10kW AC motor, Curtis 1232SE motor controller, throttle, and key. Batteries are Porsche Cayenne hybrid cells with DALY Bluetooth Smart BMS.

Balloon-Powered Car Design

College balloon car prototype

Designed and built a balloon-powered car project for first-year mechanical engineering design experience. Focused on lightweight and efficient design. Using a cardboard chassis and 3d printed wheels, my team placed 2nd in speed and 3rd in distance at the competition. The nozzle was designed in SOLIDWORKS and the frame in AutoCAD

3D Printed Replacement Cover for Thule Bike Rack

Cover printed in PETG

Back view of the bike rack when folded up

Original broken part

Designed and 3D printed a replacement cover for a Thule bike rack. The original part was broken and left internal mechanisms exposed. I used SOLIDWORKS to design a replacement part and printed it in PETG for its weather and UV resistance.
